# Business Goals

## Why are we building this website?
(Example: To get more customers online, showcase our services, and allow online bookings.)

## Top 3 Goals
1. Goal One – e.g. Generate 50 leads per month
2. Goal Two – e.g. Reduce phone enquiries by 30%
3. Goal Three – e.g. Build brand trust and credibility

## How will we measure success?
- Number of website visitors
- Number of enquiries / sign-ups
- Sales or bookings generated
