## Detailed Milestone Checklist

### Phase 1 — Planning
- [ ] Mission and vision document complete
- [ ] Business goals defined
- [ ] Target audience described
- [ ] Website requirements document finalised
- [ ] Features list agreed and signed off
- [ ] Out of scope agreed and documented
- [ ] Brand guidelines document complete
- [ ] Page layout and sitemap approved
- [ ] Content plan created
- [ ] Budget approved
- [ ] Developer and designer selected and contracted

### Phase 2 — Design
- [ ] Wireframes (page layout sketches) created by designer
- [ ] Homepage design mockup reviewed and approved
- [ ] Inner pages design mockup approved
- [ ] Mobile design reviewed and approved
- [ ] Colours, fonts, and images finalised
- [ ] Logo files delivered in correct formats

### Phase 3 — Development
- [ ] Development environment and hosting set up
- [ ] Homepage built and matches approved design
- [ ] All pages built
- [ ] Contact form built and tested
- [ ] Mobile responsive design confirmed
- [ ] All integrations connected (Analytics, WhatsApp, payment etc.)
- [ ] Website speed tested and optimised

### Phase 4 — Content Entry
- [ ] All page text added and proofread
- [ ] All images uploaded and optimised
- [ ] SEO titles and descriptions added for all pages
- [ ] Social media links added
- [ ] Contact details correct on all pages
- [ ] Business hours correct

### Phase 5 — Testing
- [ ] Tested on iPhone (Safari and Chrome)
- [ ] Tested on Android (Chrome)
- [ ] Tested on Windows desktop (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
- [ ] Tested on Mac desktop (Safari, Chrome)
- [ ] All contact forms tested — submissions arrive in inbox
- [ ] All links tested — no broken links
- [ ] Google PageSpeed score checked (target 80+)
- [ ] All images loading correctly
- [ ] Phone numbers click to call on mobile
- [ ] Email address clicks to open email app

### Phase 6 — Legal
- [ ] Privacy Policy page live on website
- [ ] Terms & Conditions page live on website
- [ ] Cookie Policy page live on website
- [ ] All three legal pages linked in footer
- [ ] Cookie consent banner working correctly
- [ ] Contact form links to Privacy Policy

### Phase 7 — Launch
- [ ] Domain name connected to website
- [ ] SSL certificate active (padlock shows in browser, site loads as https)
- [ ] Website is accessible to public
- [ ] Google Analytics confirmed receiving data
- [ ] Google Search Console sitemap submitted
- [ ] All team members notified of launch
- [ ] Launch announcement prepared (social media, email, WhatsApp)
- [ ] Launch announcement published

### Phase 8 — Post-Launch (First 30 Days)
- [ ] Website monitored daily for first week
- [ ] Feedback collected from first visitors
- [ ] Any critical bugs fixed within 48 hours
- [ ] Google Analytics reviewed after 2 weeks
- [ ] 30-day review meeting with developer scheduled
- [ ] Ongoing maintenance plan signed and confirmed
- [ ] Next set of improvements planned (nice-to-have features)

## Risks & How to Handle Them

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Prevention |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Content not ready on time | High | High | Set firm deadlines, start writing content immediately |
| Design revisions take too long | Medium | Medium | Agree maximum 2 rounds of revisions in contract |
| Developer misses deadlines | Medium | High | Milestone-based payments tied to deliverables |
| Budget overrun | Medium | High | Keep 15% contingency, approve all scope changes in writing |
| Legal documents delayed | Medium | High | Engage legal professional in Phase 1, not Phase 6 |
| Hosting or domain issues at launch | Low | High | Test domain connection 48 hours before official launch |
