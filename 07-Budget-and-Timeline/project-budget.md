## Cost Saving Tips

- Use free stock photos from Unsplash or Pexels instead of paid stock sites
- Use Google Workspace Starter (cheapest plan) for business email
- BigRock and Namecheap offer affordable Indian domain registrations
- Ask developer to include 3 months free support in the contract
- Use free analytics tools (Google Analytics, Google Search Console)
