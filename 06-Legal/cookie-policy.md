## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on a visitor's device when they visit your website.
They help your website remember visitors, count how many people visit, and show relevant content.
Cookies are not harmful but many countries require you to tell visitors about them and get their consent.

## How Visitors Can Control Cookies

Visitors can manage their cookie preferences through:
1. Our cookie consent banner shown on first visit
2. Their own web browser settings
3. Third-party opt-out tools

Useful links to include in our Cookie Policy:
- Google Analytics opt-out: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
- Facebook ad preferences: https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=ads
- All cookies: Browser settings > Privacy > Cookies
